How would you sound on Titan?

Titan’s air is colder, thicker, and slower for sound than Earth’s. Record your voice, then hear it with a lower pitch and a muffled, dense-atmosphere tone.

Why it sounds different

  • Lower pitch. Sound travels at about 194 m/s on Titan versus 343 m/s on Earth, so vocal-tract resonances drop (here, to about 62% of your original pitch).
  • Muffled. The dense nitrogen–methane air damps high frequencies, so the playback is low-passed and slightly bass-heavy.
